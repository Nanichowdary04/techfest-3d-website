    Questions

    Before you
    arrive.

    Who can take part?

    Any student from a school, college or university. Junior events are open to grades 8–12.

    Do I need a team?

    Most competitions take teams of 1–4. Solo? Join the team-matching session on Day 1 morning.

    Is there accommodation?

    Yes. Limited campus stays come as an add-on with Competitor and All-Access passes.

    Can I volunteer?

    Volunteer applications open every October. Write to us through the contact link below.
